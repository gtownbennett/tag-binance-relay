TAG Relay 2.8.7 RC6.5 — Binance-safe grading
================================================

- Removes the automatic grader's direct dependency on the geo-blocked Binance
  Futures REST kline route.
- Resolves exact-due grade prices from durable relay snapshots first, stored
  Binance Vision futures candles second, and completed-day Binance Vision
  archives third.
- Keeps the 15-minute exact-due tolerance and refuses to grade when no trusted
  Binance sample is available.
- Never silently substitutes an alternate exchange price.
- Stores auditable source provenance for every new grade.
- Normalizes seconds, milliseconds, and microseconds from Binance archives.
- Corrects a seconds/milliseconds formatting defect that could turn a valid
  2026 candle time into a 1970 timestamp and report an enormous sample offset.
- Adds four focused regressions and bumps the relay to 2.8.7-rc6.5.
